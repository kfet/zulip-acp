Rename the Zulip topic THIS conversation lives in. Use it when the topic's name does not describe what the conversation is about — most importantly when the relay opened the topic for you and auto-named it after the first line of the opening message, which is a placeholder, not a title. Write the title a human scanning the channel would want: short, specific, noun-heavy, no trailing punctuation, at most {{.MaxTopicLength}} characters. The rename is applied when your turn ends, and it moves the whole topic, so the conversation and your session follow it. It renames the topic you are in; there is no way to name another one. Call it at most once per turn, and do not mention having done it in your reply.
